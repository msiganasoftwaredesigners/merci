Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

At Merci Media & Digitals, a business line of Msigana Technologies PLC, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit mercimedia.et, request a quote, contact us, or hire us for video production, photography, and related digital content services.

By accessing our website and services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Services Covered by This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all services operated by Merci Media & Digitals, including:

  • Corporate and Brand Video Production
  • Birthday, Wedding, and Private Event Videography
  • NGO Documentaries and Awareness Campaigns
  • Social Media Video Content Packages
  • Professional Photography
  • Studio Production
  • Documentary Production
  • Multilingual Content Production
  • Video Hosting and Presentation
  • Digital Marketing and Content Creation

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you request a quote, contact us, or hire us, we may collect:

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number or WhatsApp Number
  • Email Address
  • Company or Organization Name
  • Project Description and Requirements
  • Preferred Production Language
  • Project Timeline and Budget (if provided)
  • Billing and Payment Information
  • Communication Preferences

Photos, Video, and Audio

When we film or photograph your project or event, we may capture images, video, and audio of you, your guests, your staff, or your beneficiaries. We use this material to deliver your project, and we handle it in line with the agreement or quotation we have with you. For NGO and field projects, we work with the client to make sure that people who appear on camera understand how the footage will be used.

Technical Information

We automatically collect certain technical information including:

  • IP Address
  • Browser Type
  • Device Information
  • Operating System
  • Pages Visited
  • Time Spent on Pages
  • Referral Sources

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to quote requests, inquiries, and support requests.
  • Plan, produce, edit, and deliver your video and photography projects.
  • Prepare proposals, quotations, contracts, and invoices.
  • Contact you by phone, WhatsApp, Telegram, or email about your project.
  • Improve website functionality and user experience.
  • Analyze website performance and visitor behavior.
  • Showcase our work, with your permission where required.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience, remember preferences, analyze website traffic, and enhance website performance.

  • Website Performance Monitoring
  • User Preference Storage
  • Analytics and Reporting
  • Marketing Effectiveness Measurement
  • Security and Fraud Prevention

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, some website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party providers to operate our services, such as website hosting and security providers, analytics platforms, communication tools (WhatsApp, Telegram, and email), and video platforms such as YouTube, which we use to display our work on this website.

These providers only receive information necessary to perform their services and have their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information, project files, and raw and edited footage only for as long as necessary to provide services, fulfill contractual obligations, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Restrict processing of your information.
  • Object to certain uses of your information.
  • Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Ask us to stop using photos or video of you in our portfolio or marketing.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information through it. When we film events such as birthdays and family celebrations, footage of children is captured and used only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
